Who we are

We are a French digital arts studio & creative crew located in Lyon

LABLAB is an artistic lab that offers a research and creation space for new audiovisual, immersive, and interactive experiences. Located at Pôle PIXEL, the heart of Lyon’s creative industries, it fosters exchanges between creators in film, video games, and digital arts, blending technology with the challenges of tomorrow.

Founded by Théoriz and AADN, our team of 9 companies & artists displays a wide range of creative talents. Our immersive studio is also available for rent: filming, photoshoots, virtual productions, residencies…

Our Creative Workforce

Théoriz Studio

Creative Studio

Théoriz is a creative studio designing poetic and unconventional experiences with cutting-edge technologies.

AADN – Arts & Digital Cultures

Association

AADN carries out artistic and cultural projects that challenge perceptions and inspire the desire to create a sensitive, supportive, and responsible post-digital society.

Augmenta

Tracking Technology

A complete hardware & software tracking solution that connects to all media servers and creative software.

French Film Production

Film Production

French Film Production is a company that supports businesses and institutions in the production of films in all its forms. It is active in the world of advertising and cinema, including documentaries.

Cie Corps Au Bord – Natacha Paquignon

Dance Company

Corps Au Bord is a dance and digital art company that explores the relationship between body and landscape, and body and technology.

Nicolas Guichard

Le Hoop

Nicolas Guichard is an electromechanical engineer and digital tinkerer who develops and designs devices for live performance, events and contemporary art.

Alex Andrix

Arts & Science

Alex Andrix is a digital and contemporary artist for Science. He generates abstract paintings, scientific data visualizations, illustrations, films and VR experiences.

Julie Borgeot

Culture · Data · Citizenship

An independent engineer and expert in participatory design for culture and education, she analyses and visualises cultural data and creates outreach tools.

Danaël Valbert

Artist & Inventor

Danaël Valbert is an artist involved in creative lute-making and wood crafting. He is the creator of the Kebab instrument and has composed Kebab symphonies.
