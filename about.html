About LABLAB

Who we are

A gathering of independent talents

LABLAB is an artistic lab in Villeurbanne, at Pôle PIXEL, the heart of the Lyon area’s creative industries. It brings together independent artists, engineers and small companies who share spaces, tools and skills, and offer their services together in immersive, audiovisual and interactive production. Meet the members.

The lab fosters exchanges between creators from film, video games and digital arts, blending technology with the questions of tomorrow.

AADN – Arts & Digital Cultures

Founded in Lyon in 2004, AADN carries an artistic, cultural and political project rooted in encounters between arts, sciences and technologies. A civil-society actor and cultural entrepreneur, it works in the field of social, technological and artistic innovation.

Together, Théoriz and AADN manage LABLAB day to day.

What we share

  • A 100 m² immersive studio, ready for mixed reality
  • A build workshop
  • A creativity room
  • A co-working space
  • Tools, technologies and the skills that go with them

LABLAB also hosts outside projects in production or filming with artistic, scientific or technological research stakes, through residencies, co-design, training, workshops and demonstrations.
