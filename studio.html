Immersive Studio

Immersive Cube

A 100 m² immersive studio

At the heart of LABLAB, our cubic immersive studio is ready for mixed reality. Video mapping on walls and floor, real-time tracking and real-time graphics make it a place for experimentation as much as production.

What it’s used for

Filming & photoshoots

Shoots and photo sessions in an immersive, fully controllable environment.

Virtual production

Real-time backgrounds projected around performers and objects, captured in camera.

Performance & dance

An interactive stage for choreographers and performers, with full-body tracking.

Residencies & workshops

Artists in residence develop and test new immersive works.

Technology

The studio runs and improves thanks to our technical partners:

  • Immersive XP designer: Théoriz
  • Sensing technology: Augmenta
  • Real-time graphics: TouchDesigner by Derivative
  • Real-time graphics: Notch
  • Video mapping software: HeavyM
  • Media server: Smode
  • Calibration: VIOSO
  • Videoprojectors: Epson
