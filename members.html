Meet us

Our Creative Workforce

LABLAB gathers independent artists, engineers and small companies at Pôle PIXEL, Lyon. Each works under their own name and can be hired directly, or together with other members for larger projects, including in our immersive studio.

Théoriz Studio

Creative Studio

Théoriz is a creative studio designing poetic and unconventional experiences with cutting-edge technologies. A founding member of LABLAB, it co-manages the lab and its immersive studio.

Visit website

AADN – Arts & Digital Cultures

Association

Founded in Lyon in 2004, AADN carries an artistic, cultural and political project rooted in encounters between arts, sciences and technologies. A civil-society actor and cultural entrepreneur, it works in the field of social, technological and artistic innovation, and runs residency programs and workshops for digital artists. AADN is a founding member of LABLAB.

Augmenta

Tracking Technology

Augmenta is a plug-and-play tracking solution for anyone who wants to bring interactivity into a space: theme parks, museums, urban environments or stages. Its hardware and software connect to all media servers and creative software.

Cie Corps Au Bord – Natacha Paquignon

Dance Company

Corps Au Bord is a dance and digital art company exploring the relationship between body and landscape, and between body and technology. It creates augmented-reality choreographies and stage performances that place the body at the root of a possible dialogue between humans and machines.
